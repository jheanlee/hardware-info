# 0.2.0

- Added the `battery` feature.
- Added `Battery`, `Batteries`, `BatteryChargeStatus` and `BatteryChemistry`, along with their associated functions.

# 0.1.0

- Added support for FreeBSD, Linux, macOS, NetBSD and Windows.
- Added the `pci` feature.
- Added `PciDevice`, `PciDevices`, `PciAddress`, `PciDeviceClass` and `PciBusSpeed`, along with their associated
  functions.
